How it works

Four steps, about ten minutes.

Counselors have limited appointment slots. This gets the mechanical part — what's done, what's left, what order it goes in — figured out before you sit down, so the appointment is spent on judgment calls.

STEP 01

Pick your major and campuses

Type any major — the planner matches it to a known template or builds one. Then choose the specific campuses you're applying to, from all 9 UC and 23 CSU campuses.

STEP 02

Enter what you've finished

Check off courses from your transcript, mark anything in progress, and set your transfer term and unit load.

STEP 03

Edit the draft, then download

Move courses between terms while the audit updates live, then export a PDF with your term grid and questions for your counselor.

Read this before you rely on it. TransferTracker uses common C-ID course descriptors and the Cal-GETC pattern to build a draft. Course numbering, articulation, and impaction rules vary by college and campus, and they change every year. Always verify your agreement on assist.org and confirm the plan with a counselor at your college. This is a conversation starter, not an official Student Educational Plan.
